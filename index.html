Privacy Policy

Personal Health Record (PHR) — an open-source, self-hosted application

Last updated: August 6, 2026

PHR is self-hosted software, not a hosted service. The project's authors and contributors do not operate a central server, do not have access to any instance's data, and do not receive, process, or store any personal or health information themselves.

Each installation of PHR is deployed and controlled by the person or organization who runs it (the "operator") — often you, for your own or your family's use. The operator determines where data is stored, who can access it, and how long it is kept. This policy explains what data the PHR software itself collects and how it handles that data technically; it is not a substitute for whatever policy your specific operator may additionally provide.

What data PHR stores

When you use a PHR instance, the following data is stored by that instance's operator, in the database and file storage the operator controls:

  • Account information: email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Family and patient profiles: names and other profile details you enter for yourself and family members whose records you manage.
  • Medical documents: images and PDFs you upload (e.g. prescriptions, lab reports, bills, notes), along with automatically generated thumbnails and best-effort OCR text extracted from those documents.
  • Usage metadata: timestamps and an audit log of actions such as uploads, edits, and admin actions, used for accountability and troubleshooting within the instance.

Where this data goes

  • All data is stored on infrastructure chosen by the operator — typically a self-managed server, homelab, or private cloud instance. The PHR software does not use any third-party object storage, analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service by default.
  • Uploaded files are served back to your web browser or mobile app through short-lived, cryptographically signed links generated by the operator's own API — not through any external CDN or third party.
  • Data is only shared with other people to the extent the account holder explicitly grants access — for example, by adding a family member to a shared family group. PHR does not sell, rent, or share data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Mobile app permissions

The PHR mobile app may request the following device permissions, used solely to let you capture and manage your own documents:

  • Camera and photo library: to capture or select photos of medical documents for upload.
  • Microphone (Android): this permission is declared by a bundled library used for media capture; PHR does not record audio or access the microphone for any feature.

The app does not access contacts, location, or any other device data, and does not include any advertising or analytics SDK.

Data retention and deletion

Because each instance is self-hosted, retention and deletion are controlled by the operator and the account holder. Within the app, users can delete individual records; deleted records are removed from view and their underlying files are eventually cleaned up by the operator's storage. Full account or data deletion is performed by the operator (for example, by removing your account or restoring from a backup that excludes your data).

Security

PHR uses password hashing, authenticated sessions, and time-limited signed URLs for file access. Records are private by default and only visible to accounts you explicitly grant access to. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and overall security also depends on how an operator configures and maintains their instance (e.g. keeping the software updated, using HTTPS, and securing the host).

Children's privacy

PHR is a family record-keeping tool and may store profiles for minors created and managed by a parent or guardian's account. PHR itself does not knowingly collect data directly from children, and does not offer public registration targeted at children.

Open source

PHR's full source code is publicly available for review at github.com/ashokkumarin/personal-health-record, including exactly how data is stored, transmitted, and secured.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the PHR software evolves. Changes will be published at this same URL with an updated "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about the PHR software or this policy can be raised by opening an issue on the project's GitHub issue tracker. If you have a question about a specific instance's data, please contact that instance's operator directly.
