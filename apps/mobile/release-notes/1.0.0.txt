Initial release of PHR — a private, self-hosted home for your family's health records.

- Store prescriptions, lab reports, bills, and notes in one place
- Timeline view with search and filtering
- Upload photos or PDFs of documents, with automatic thumbnails
- Family groups with per-patient profiles
- Records stay private by default
- Connects to your own self-hosted PHR server — no third-party cloud storage
- Configurable background sync (1–180 min)
